About ColOurs

A place where everyone belongs.

Our name brings together Color, Ours, and Colors—a reminder that every identity, background, and mind belongs to all of us.

ColOurs is a welcoming place to understand discrimination, learn from perspectives different from our own, and help build a more thoughtful, accepting community.

Our promise

To make room for curiosity, dignity, and meaningful change.
